## Overview

Resurrection Tech Ltd welcomes partnerships, referrals, sponsorships,
integrations, and strategic collaboration. We work with introducers,
enterprise sales partners, procurement teams, dashboard / UI
implementers, HITL operations vendors, compliance specialists, advisory
firms, integrators, and brand sponsors.

Every engagement is designed to be productive for both sides — and every
engagement respects a single, non-negotiable distinction:

> **Compensation and ownership are separate concepts.** A partner can be
> paid for contributing to an opportunity without acquiring any
> ownership of, or rights in, the underlying framework, methodology, or
> recurring revenue streams.

This document sets out, in plain terms, **what partners do**, **how
partners get paid**, and **what partners do not automatically receive**
by virtue of contributing to an engagement.

## What Partners Do **Not** Automatically Receive

Contributing to an engagement — whether by referral, sales support,
implementation, sponsorship, or any other service — does **not** grant a
partner any of the following:

- **Ownership rights** in the Morrison Framework™, the governance
  engine, the audit methodology, or any derivative
- **Licensing rights** — commercial, enterprise, sublicensing,
  redistribution, or managed-service rights
- **Audit methodology rights** — no entitlement to deliver, white-label,
  or productise the Runtime Governance Audit™
- **Patent rights** — no express or implied licence to UK Patent
  GB2600765.8 or any related filing
- **Recurring revenue rights** — no entitlement to governance
  subscription revenue, renewals, or ongoing engagement revenue beyond
  the partner's own contracted services
- **Perpetual participation rights** — no entitlement to be included in
  future deals, follow-on engagements, or successor work
- **Sublicensing rights** — no right to extend, transfer, or grant any
  rights to a third party
- **Rights to the governance engine** itself — the runtime reachability
  core and its evaluation framework remain solely with Resurrection Tech
  Ltd

A partner is compensated for the **services they provide**, not for the
intellectual property those services touch.

## Partner Revenue Independence

Partners are encouraged to **develop and price their own services
independently**.

Resurrection Tech Ltd does **not** cap, control, or dictate the pricing
of partner services. Partners may charge **market rates** for the work
they perform, including:

- implementation,
- advisory,
- compliance,
- maintenance,
- support,
- training,
- operational services.

This freedom applies on one condition: **those services must not
represent ownership of, or rights in,** the Morrison Framework™, the
Runtime Governance Audit™, the governance engine, the patents, the
licensing rights, or any recurring governance subscription. A partner
sets the price of *its own* work; it does not set the price of, or
acquire any share in, Resurrection Tech's intellectual property or
governance revenue.

In short: **price your own services as you see fit; the core asset
remains Resurrection Tech's.**

## Payment Models

Engagements use one of three models. The model is selected per
engagement and stated explicitly in the written agreement.

### Option A — Client Pays Partner Directly

The partner invoices the client separately for its own services
(implementation, dashboards, HITL operations, advisory, maintenance,
workshops, integration). Resurrection Tech does not collect or
intermediate the partner's fee, and the partner does not collect or
intermediate Resurrection Tech's fee.

### Option B — Resurrection Tech Pays Partner

Resurrection Tech pays the partner an agreed **referral fee**,
**success fee**, or **project fee** — typically for introductions, sales
support, procurement support, or specific work commissioned by
Resurrection Tech. Amount, trigger, and timing are defined in writing
before the engagement begins.

### Option C — Hybrid

The client pays the partner directly for operational services
(implementation, HITL, advisory, maintenance) **and** Resurrection Tech
pays the partner a referral or success bonus where applicable. Both
arms are defined in writing and operate independently.

In all three models, **Resurrection Tech remains the sole counterparty
for the Runtime Governance Audit™, the licence, and any recurring
governance subscription.**

## Deal Origination Examples

The following illustrate how compensation flows in common scenarios.
They are examples, not commitments — actual terms are always agreed in
writing.

### Example 1 — Resurrection Tech Finds and Closes the Client

Resurrection Tech sources the opportunity, runs the conversation, and
closes the engagement directly.

- Resurrection Tech keeps **100%** of audit, licensing, and governance
  revenue.
- **No** referral fee or success bonus applies.
- A partner may still be engaged separately (e.g. for implementation,
  HITL operations, or dashboards) under the partner's own contract with
  the client (Option A) — that work is independent of the audit /
  licence revenue.

### Example 2 — Partner Finds the Client, Resurrection Tech Closes

The partner sources the introduction; Resurrection Tech runs the
commercial close and delivers the audit / licence.

- The partner may receive an agreed **referral fee** — e.g. **10%, 20%,
  or 25% of first-contract value** — agreed in writing **before**
  execution.
- The referral fee applies to the introduced engagement only and is not
  perpetual.
- **Audit, licensing, and governance revenue remain Resurrection Tech
  revenue.** The partner does not acquire ownership, licensing rights,
  or recurring revenue rights by virtue of the introduction.

### Example 3 — Client Buys from Both Companies Separately

The client engages both parties for distinct work.

- **Resurrection Tech invoices** the client for the Runtime Governance
  Audit™, the licence, and any governance subscription.
- **The partner invoices** the client separately for implementation,
  dashboard / UI work, HITL setup and operations, advisory / vCAIO,
  workshops, integration, or maintenance.
- Each party owns its own contract, scope, deliverables, and revenue.
  Neither contract grants the other party rights in the other's work.

## Written Agreement Requirement

Every compensation arrangement — referral fee, success fee, project
fee, day-rate, retainer, sponsorship, or any other payment — **must be
agreed in writing before work begins.**

A written agreement, signed by Resurrection Tech Ltd, defines at
minimum:

- the scope of work,
- the deliverables,
- the compensation (amount, structure, currency, payment trigger,
  timing),
- the term and termination conditions,
- the confirmation that the engagement does not transfer or grant
  ownership, licensing, audit-methodology, patent, recurring-revenue, or
  sublicensing rights.

No verbal agreement, implied arrangement, prior course of dealing,
informal exchange, expectation set in conversation, or pattern of past
referrals creates any entitlement to payment, ownership, licensing, or
recurring revenue participation. Work undertaken without a prior
written agreement is undertaken at the partner's sole risk and creates
no obligation on Resurrection Tech Ltd.
