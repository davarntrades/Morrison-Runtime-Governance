## Pricing Principle

Morrison Runtime Governance is **catastrophic-trajectory prevention
infrastructure** — a structural governance layer that makes forbidden
executable states unreachable before any action occurs. It is not AI
consulting, AI tooling, or an assistant-safety add-on. It is operational
containment architecture for autonomous systems.

The price of the layer is a fraction of what happens when Ω is reached. Each
deployment is priced by the **cost of the failure it prevents** — not by the

## Why the pricing scales

Pricing is proportional to four factors, in order of weight:

| Factor                       | What it measures                                                            |
|:-----------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Operational blast radius** | How much the system can move/destroy in one unsafe trajectory (money, data, infrastructure) |
| **Regulatory exposure**      | FCA / SOX / HIPAA / GDPR consequence of a single reachable Ω state           |
| **Infrastructure criticality**| Whether the autonomous system is load-bearing for the organisation or sovereign function |
| **Catastrophic downside**    | Whether failure is recoverable, insurable, or terminal                       |

**The governance layer is priced against the cost of Ω becoming reachable —
not the complexity of the software.** A deployment that contains a system able
to move billions, expose a regulated population, or affect sovereign
infrastructure is priced against that downside, because that downside is what
the layer structurally removes.

The documented-cost figures below are external references that bound the
*downside* the layer removes; they are not claims about this software.

**Cost of Ω:** existential; unquantifiable in financial terms. Autonomous
coordination, classified handling, sovereign runtime governance.

| Failure mode                          | Consequence                                                          |
|:--------------------------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Autonomous weapon constraint violation| Catastrophic escalation. Civilian casualties. International incident. |
