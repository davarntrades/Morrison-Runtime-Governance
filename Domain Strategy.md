## 1. Autonomous Robotics

**Ontology fit: very strong.**

Robots are dynamical systems executing trajectories inside physical environments. The framework maps directly onto:

|Ω Region                     |What It Prevents                                   |
|:----------------------------|:--------------------------------------------------|
|Forbidden motion regions     |Collision with humans, infrastructure, other robots|
|Unsafe actuator sequences    |Overcurrent, overextension, mechanical failure     |
|Human proximity constraints  |Safety zone violations in collaborative workspaces |
|Irreversible physical actions|Destructive operations without confirmation        |

**Target verticals:** warehouse robotics, drones, autonomous manufacturing, surgical robotics, defence robotics.

**Why it converts:** These systems already have safety envelopes. The framework formalises them as Ω and enforces them at the trajectory level rather than relying on sensor-reactive emergency stops.

**Pricing range:** £500K–2M+ (Enterprise Autonomous Systems — autonomous robotics)

## 3. Multi-Agent Coordination

**The most important future direction.**

Current safety research assumes one agent. Future systems involve many agents, tool-sharing, delegated tasks, recursive workflows, and autonomous coordination.

|Threat                      |What the Framework Catches                                    |
|:---------------------------|:-------------------------------------------------------------|
|Chain escalation            |Agent A grants permissions that Agent B exploits              |
|Recursive delegation        |Task delegated through N agents accumulates unsafe capability |
|Distributed unsafe execution|No single agent violates Ω but the combined trajectory does   |
|Emergent unsafe behaviour   |Coordination produces trajectories no individual agent planned|

**Why it matters:** Multi-agent safety cannot be solved by aligning individual agents. It requires trajectory-level governance over the emergent system. The enforcement hierarchy (A_safe → V5) already supports this — V5 evaluates invariant stability across environment sets ℰ, which includes multi-agent perturbation.

**Pricing range:** £500K–2M+ (Enterprise Autonomous Systems — multi-agent coordination)
